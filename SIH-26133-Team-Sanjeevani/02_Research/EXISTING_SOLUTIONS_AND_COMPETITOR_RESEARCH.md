# Existing Solutions and Competitor Research

## SIH Problem Statement ID
26133

---

## Purpose

This document studies existing digital healthcare solutions and identifies useful features, limitations and opportunities for our proposed solution.

---

## Existing Solutions

| Solution | Main Focus | Useful Features | Gap / Opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|

| eSanjeevani | Government telemedicine platform | Remote doctor consultation and teleconsultation support | Mainly focused on consultation; our opportunity is to connect consultation with patient journey, referrals, follow-ups and facility coordination |

| Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission (ABDM) | Digital health ecosystem and interoperable health records | Digital health IDs, health records and interoperability | Our opportunity is to demonstrate how interoperable records can support the complete rural patient journey, including triage, referrals and follow-ups |

| Practo | Digital healthcare platform | Doctor discovery, appointment booking and online consultations | Our opportunity is to focus specifically on rural public healthcare workflows, frontline health workers and continuity across government healthcare facilities |

| Tata 1mg | Digital healthcare and medicine services | Medicine information, online consultations and health-related services | Our opportunity is to focus on public healthcare coordination, rural access and medicine availability visibility across healthcare facilities |

| Aarogya Setu | Government digital health platform | Health information, alerts and public health support | Our opportunity is to provide a more complete care-coordination workflow focused on patients, frontline workers, referrals and follow-ups |

---

## Key Findings

The research shows that existing solutions address individual healthcare needs such as teleconsultation, digital health records, appointments or medicine services.

Our opportunity is to combine these important features into one connected workflow focused specifically on rural and underserved public healthcare.

### Key Differentiator

**A connected rural healthcare coordination platform that supports the complete patient journey:**

Patient Registration → Triage → Consultation → Referral → Follow-up → Facility Monitoring
