# Proposed Solution – Sanjeevani

## Core Idea
Sanjeevani creates a connected patient-care journey instead of isolated healthcare interactions.

## How It Works
1. A patient is registered and basic information is captured.
2. Symptoms and vitals are recorded by the relevant user or healthcare worker.
3. The prototype applies rule-based triage to support priority classification.
4. The patient is guided toward consultation or referral when required.
5. Referral status and follow-up activities are tracked.
6. Role-based users see information relevant to their responsibilities.

## Accessibility Innovation
The prototype includes voice-assisted interaction and multilingual support to reduce dependence on complex text-based navigation.

## Key Value
Sanjeevani focuses on **connected records, tracked referrals, timely follow-ups, accessible interaction and decision support**.
