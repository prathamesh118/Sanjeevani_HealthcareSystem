# Stakeholder Map and User Personas

## SIH Problem Statement ID
26133

## Project
Accessibility and Quality of Public Healthcare Services in Rural and Underserved Areas

---

## 1. Purpose

This document identifies the key stakeholders involved in the rural public healthcare ecosystem and defines the primary users of the proposed system.

The stakeholder analysis helps us understand who interacts with the healthcare system, what their responsibilities are, what problems they face and how the proposed solution can support them.

---

## 2. Stakeholder Categories

The stakeholders are divided into the following categories:

### Primary Stakeholders
These users directly interact with or benefit from the proposed system.

### Secondary Stakeholders
These users support, manage or coordinate healthcare services.

### Institutional Stakeholders
These stakeholders are responsible for healthcare administration, governance and the public healthcare system.

---

## 4. User Personas

The following user personas represent the main users who are expected to interact directly with the proposed system. These personas will help guide the design of features, workflows and user interfaces.

### Persona 1 – Rural Patient

**User Type:** Primary User

**Goals:**
- Receive timely healthcare support.
- Reduce unnecessary travel and waiting time.
- Understand the next steps in the healthcare journey.
- Receive proper follow-up care.

**Current Problems:**
- May need to travel long distances to access healthcare facilities.
- May experience long waiting times.
- May face language and health literacy barriers.
- May need to visit multiple facilities without continuity of information.

**Needs from the System:**
- Simple and easy-to-understand interaction.
- Multilingual support.
- Assistance from healthcare workers where required.
- Better appointment and referral coordination.
- Continuity of healthcare information.

**Interaction with the System:**
The patient may interact with the system directly or with assistance from a frontline healthcare worker to access healthcare services, appointments, referrals and follow-up information.

### Persona 2 – ASHA Worker / Frontline Healthcare Worker

**User Type:** Primary User

**Goals:**
- Help patients access appropriate healthcare services.
- Record basic patient information efficiently.
- Identify patients who may require urgent attention.
- Coordinate referrals and follow-up activities.

**Current Problems:**
- May rely on manual or fragmented patient information.
- May have difficulty tracking patients across multiple healthcare visits.
- May need to coordinate between patients and different healthcare facilities.
- May work in areas with unreliable internet connectivity.

**Needs from the System:**
- Simple patient registration.
- Easy recording of symptoms and basic health information.
- Digital support for patient prioritization.
- Referral and follow-up tracking.
- Offline or low-connectivity support.
- Multilingual and easy-to-use interfaces.

**Interaction with the System:**
The ASHA Worker or frontline healthcare worker acts as an important bridge between the patient and the public healthcare system. They can use the system to register patients, record basic information, support triage, coordinate consultations, track referrals and manage follow-up activities.

### Persona 3 – Doctor

**User Type:** Primary User

**Goals:**
- Quickly understand the patient's relevant health information.
- Provide timely consultation and medical guidance.
- Identify cases that require referral or urgent attention.
- Reduce repeated collection of the same patient information.

**Current Problems:**
- Patient information may be fragmented across different healthcare facilities.
- Complete medical history may not always be available during consultation.
- High patient workload can affect consultation efficiency.
- Referral and follow-up status may not always be clearly visible.

**Needs from the System:**
- Access to relevant longitudinal patient records.
- Clear summary of symptoms and basic health information.
- Patient priority or triage information for decision support.
- Referral creation and tracking.
- Visibility of previous consultations and follow-up information.

**Interaction with the System:**
The doctor uses the system to review relevant patient information, provide consultation, make referral decisions when required, update patient care information and review follow-up status.

### Persona 4 – Healthcare Administrator

**User Type:** Institutional User

**Goals:**
- Monitor healthcare service delivery and facility performance.
- Identify delays and gaps in patient care workflows.
- Monitor referrals, follow-ups and high-risk cases.
- Improve healthcare quality and accountability.

**Current Problems:**
- Healthcare and operational information may be scattered across different systems or processes.
- It may be difficult to get a unified view of patient flow and service performance.
- Referral completion and follow-up status may not always be clearly visible.
- Identifying recurring facility-level issues can be difficult without structured data.

**Needs from the System:**
- Facility-level dashboards.
- Visibility of patient flow and service activity.
- Referral and follow-up monitoring.
- High-risk patient indicators.
- Key performance and quality indicators.

**Interaction with the System:**
The healthcare administrator uses dashboards and monitoring tools to understand facility performance, patient flow, referral status, follow-up activities and other indicators that support healthcare quality and accountability.

## 5. Key Stakeholder and Persona Insights

The stakeholder and persona analysis shows that the proposed solution must support multiple users with different responsibilities, needs and levels of technical knowledge.

The most important insight is that the system should not be designed as a patient-only application. It should act as a coordinated healthcare support platform connecting patients, frontline healthcare workers, doctors, healthcare facilities and administrators.

The system should therefore provide role-based workflows, simple user interfaces and appropriate access to information based on each user's responsibilities. Special attention should be given to frontline healthcare workers, who act as an important connection between rural communities and the public healthcare system.

These stakeholder insights will be used in the next phases to design the system workflow, features, user interfaces and technical architecture.
