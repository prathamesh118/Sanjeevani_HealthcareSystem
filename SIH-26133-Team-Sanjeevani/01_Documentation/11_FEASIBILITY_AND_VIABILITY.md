# Feasibility and Viability – Sanjeevani

## Technical Feasibility
The prototype uses modern, widely available web technologies and a modular role-based design, making continued development technically practical.

## Economic Feasibility
Open-source development tools can reduce initial software costs. Production deployment, infrastructure, security and healthcare integration would require additional investment.

## User and Operational Viability
Role-based workflows support patients, healthcare workers, doctors and administrators. Voice assistance and multilingual interaction can improve accessibility, while real-world adoption will require training and workflow alignment.

## Key Challenges and Mitigation
- **Connectivity:** support offline capture and later synchronization in future versions.
- **Data and voice accuracy:** validate inputs and confirm important commands.
- **Privacy and security:** apply authentication, role-based access and audit controls in production.
- **Language and adoption:** use simple interaction and regional language support.
