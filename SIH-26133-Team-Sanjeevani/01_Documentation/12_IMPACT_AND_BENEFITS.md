# Impact and Benefits – Sanjeevani

## Patients and Caregivers
- Reduced travel burden through better care guidance.
- Better continuity through a connected care journey.
- Timely follow-ups and clearer next steps.
- Improved accessibility through voice and multilingual interaction.
- Greater health awareness and understanding of care actions.

## Healthcare Workers and Doctors
- Faster access to relevant patient information.
- Improved coordination across patient journeys.
- Better tracking of referrals and follow-ups.
- Priority-based attention to urgent cases.
- Reduced effort caused by fragmented information.

## Administrators and Health Systems
- Better visibility of healthcare workflow activity.
- Improved resource and service planning.
- Clearer tracking and accountability.
- Identification of care gaps and follow-up needs.
- Better support for data-informed coordination.
